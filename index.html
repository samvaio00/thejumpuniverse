The Jump Universe

A guide to what is actually happening overhead — and when you can see it from where you are.

In development

  • Sky events — eclipses, meteor showers, conjunctions and occultations, with local circumstances
  • Aurora — live geomagnetic activity and viewing latitude
  • Tonight — planets, moon phase, twilight windows and satellite passes for your location
Looking for observing conditions?

Whether tonight is actually usable — cloud by layer, transparency, seeing, moonlight and true darkness — is covered at GoodSeeing.

Positions are computed from published astronomical algorithms and public observational data. Nothing here is generated speculatively.
